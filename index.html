Every company in your Tracxn export, checked

Each company was looked up on the web. Every excluded company was then searched a second time with a product-focused query, which surfaced 54 more brands. Rows are added, already in the stack, or excluded with the reason. Click an added row to open its card.

How to read it. Pipeline holds startups that are unfunded or whose last public round was before Nov 2025. Funded in last 12 months holds rounds from Nov 2025 onwards. Colours mark seven sector clusters. Tracxn categories are mapped from each startup's sector and product, not pulled from Tracxn. Amounts in $ are converted at about ₹8.8 Cr per $1M for sorting. Every card links the news source for its round. Websites were found by search, so check each one before you send outreach. Startups marked with revenue come from your Tracxn seed-stage export (funding up to $2M). Their revenue, valuation, team size and round dates are Tracxn's figures, and their descriptions and websites were matched by search.
